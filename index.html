own-ai

Simple, private, GPU-free PDF maker. Upload an image or PDF, add a note with a short instruction, and download a PDF. Your file stays in your browser.

PNG, JPG, or PDF · up to 20 MB

Supported: Add text: your note, Write: your note, or Highlight for review. Notes are added visibly to the first page; existing content is not changed.

This is a rule-based document tool, not a trained AI model. It does not edit underlying identity fields or produce altered official credentials. PDF export uses the open-source pdf-lib library.
